# Lore

LODESTAR is a student-built satellite ground station. Its uplink key lets whoever holds it command
the cubesat. The key was rotated for an audit in March. The lab's field units report their status
as short DNS lookups when they have no other link — a trick Ilse built for storm nights.

Rumour in the corridor says Prof. Rao wanted Ilse off the grant. Rumour also says Dana lets people
borrow her badge. Neither rumour is the whole story.
