# CASE 017 — The Last Transmission

Codename: LODESTAR

At 03:14 on the night of 14 March, the Halvorsen Institute radio club logged a short burst on the
lab's ground-station frequency. By morning, **Dr. Ilse Varga** — lead researcher on the LODESTAR
satellite ground station at the **Kestrel Robotics Lab** — had vanished. Her workstation in
**Server Room B-114** had been wiped. A storm had cut mains power across B-wing for most of the night.

Three people had access to B-114: **Theo Marsh**, **Dana Whitlock** and **Prof. Anand Rao**.

The investigation begins with a seemingly harmless internal portal.

## Acts

1. The Message — decode what the radio club heard.
2. The Terminal — the lab's intranet remembers more than it shows.
3. The Evidence — photographs, recordings, logs and a locked backup.
4. The Machine — repository history, a packet capture, the facilities database.
5. The Inquest — interviews, the timeline, the board.
6. Campus — go where the transmission points.
7. The Last Transmission — reconstruct the night.

## Timeline

- 01:10 — Dana Whitlock's car leaves campus through the North Gate.
- 02:31 — The storm knocks out mains power in B-wing; the UPS carries the servers.
- 02:47 — B-114 opens on Dana Whitlock's badge. Minutes later the workstation is wiped.
- 03:14 — The last transmission.

## Truth

- Culprit: Theo Marsh
- Motive: agreed to hand LODESTAR ground-station access to Corvid Aerospace to clear his debts
- Method: used Dana Whitlock's borrowed badge and wiped Varga's workstation while the storm masked the outage alerts
- Location: Server Room B-114
- Time: 02:47
